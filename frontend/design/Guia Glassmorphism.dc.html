Background uses a soft category-color gradient (not flat), symbol in near-pure white for max contrast, number/name get more weight and opacity, atomic mass moved to the corner as a secondary value. Hover adds a slight lift.

Update — brighter cells on dark bg: raise the gradient's top-stop alpha to ~0.40 (was ~0.22) and bottom-stop to ~0.16, bump the border alpha to ~0.75 and lighten it, add a soft outer colored glow (0 6px 18px in the category hue at ~25% alpha) plus a 1px colored ring, and lift name/mass text opacity a notch. This keeps cells feeling lit instead of sinking into the black background.
